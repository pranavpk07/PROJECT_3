## 🎯 Objective

To analyze **one month of post-level social media data** from three major electric vehicle (EV) brands — **Tata, Mahindra, and BYD** — and extract actionable insights to support internal decision-making for our newly formed **Motar EV** company.

> **Goal:** Explore social media trends to uncover insights that guide strategic business, marketing, and product decisions.

---

## 📱 Platforms Analyzed

| Platform | Focus |
|----------|-------|
| 📸 **Instagram** | Visual content, Reels, Stories |
| ▶️ **YouTube** | Long-form reviews, explainers |
| 👥 **Facebook** | Community, family audience |
| 🐦 **Twitter (X)** | Real-time news, launches |

---

## 🧠 Key Insights

### 1️⃣ Content Timing Optimization

- ⏰ **Peak Hours:** Post between **2 PM – 7 PM**, especially around **6 PM**
- 📅 **Ideal Campaign Window:** **April 6–20** (high user activity period)

---

### 2️⃣ Platform-Specific Strategy

| Platform | Audience | Strategic Focus |
|----------|----------|-----------------|
| 📸 **Instagram** | 18–30 | Reels, Stories in Hinglish/English · Regional influencer collabs |
| ▶️ **YouTube** | 18–50 | Regional-language explainers, car reviews, tech content |
| 👥 **Facebook** | 25–45+ | Hindi/regional visuals, family-focused · Safety & efficiency messaging |
| 🐦 **Twitter (X)** | Media & Influencers | Real-time news, launches, commentary & engagement |

---

### 3️⃣ Content Format & Engagement

- ✅ Prioritize **short-form, visually rich content** (Reels, infographics)
- ✅ Promote **interactive content** (polls, Q&A, challenges)
- ✅ Focus on **engagement rate per follower** over raw impressions

#### 🔥 Top Engagement Themes by Brand

| Brand | Top Engagement Theme |
|-------|----------------------|
| 🔵 **BYD** | Driving experience, SUV buzz |
| 🔴 **Mahindra** | Booking experience, emotional engagement |
| 🟢 **Tata** | Premium positioning, high viral potential via neutral posts |

> 📹 **Video content** is the most effective format for driving engagement across all brands.

---

## 💡 Actionable Recommendations

### 📌 Marketing, Research & Insights

- Use **Instagram** for aspirational, Gen-Z focused lifestyle branding
- Leverage **regional influencers** for pan-India reach
- Deploy call-to-actions: *"Tag a friend"*, *"Drop a comment"*, etc.
- Track **applause**, **conversation**, and **amplification** metrics separately
